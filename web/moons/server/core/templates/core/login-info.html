How to sign in to the MOONS Science Archive

The sign-in page is provided by CILogon, which manages secure access on our behalf. To use the service, you must first select an identity provider (IdP) — the organisation where you have an account (your university, institute, or employer). The page lists many providers, so here is what to do:

  • Choose your institution (recommended). The page usually opens showing ORCID as the selected IdP, but for archive access you should normally pick your home institution instead. Click the small down-triangle icon (▼) on the selector to open the list of identity providers.
  • Find your organisation. Type the name of your university or institute in the search box, e.g. “University of Edinburgh” or “Science and Technology Facilities Council”, and select it from the results. If it isn't listed, CILogon's How to Select an Identity Provider guide (also linked on the sign-in page) explains the alternatives — or contact your local IT support or the MOONS Science Archive team.
  • Tick “remember this selection” if you want to skip the welcome page on subsequent visits and go straight to your chosen provider. This sets a cookie; to change it later, clear your browser's cookies and return.
  • Click “Log On” to continue to your institution's normal sign-in page and log in with your usual credentials.
  • When to use ORCID (or Google): Only use a social provider such as ORCID or Google if you specifically have an account there that you want to use. For most users, the home institution is the correct choice.
